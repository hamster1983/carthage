Carthage Group manages a range of services at such travel destinations as Tunisia, UAE, Egypt, and Tanzania (Zanzibar)

We’ve been working in the tourism industry for more than 20 years, so now we have enough ambitions, experience, competence, as well as organizational and technical resources to meet any needs and requirements.

We offer favourable terms of cooperation to tour operators, travel agencies and FIT, Group & MICE specialists. Partners appreciate the advantages of working with Carthage Group, as completing the most unusual and challenging tasks is our corporate identity.

The group includes:

Hotel chain in Tunisia B2B online booking system MICE tourism BIO cosmetics Employment abroad

International group of companies Carthage Group

Full ground handling services for companies and tourists
